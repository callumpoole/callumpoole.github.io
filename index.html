Background

I'm a game programmer based in Sheffield, UK, with a First-Class Honours degree in Games Computing from the University of Lincoln.

I have several years of professional experience developing games across a range of genres, including puzzle games, shooters and fighting games. Having worked on five titles and multiple prototypes, I've gained experience across various aspects of game development, with a particular focus on gameplay programming and systems development.

My primary experience is with C++ and Unreal Engine, although I've also worked extensively with C# and Unity. Throughout my career, I've been involved in developing gameplay mechanics, implementing core systems, prototyping features and solving complex technical challenges.

My career has taken me from the UK to Bangkok, Thailand, before returning to Sheffield, where I continue to develop games and explore new technologies and programming techniques.

Procedurally Generated Medieval World (2017)

A procedural world generator developed in Unity, using Perlin noise to generate terrain based on configurable parameters. The resulting world features a medieval castle and surrounding village, alongside forests, farmland and a lake.

Terrain modification is handled through a custom brush system inspired by Photoshop, allowing features such as hills and flattened farmland to be sculpted programmatically. All assets and systems were created by myself independently.
