Background

Hi, I'm Callum, a passionate game programmer based in Sheffield, UK. With a keen eye for detail and a drive to create immersive and innovative gaming experiences, I strive to push the boundaries of game development. I earned a First-Class degree in Games Computing from the University of Lincoln, providing me with a strong technical foundation for my career in the gaming industry.

Over the years, I've had the privilege of working on a diverse range of games, from puzzle and shooter titles to fighting games. I take pride in my ability to bring complex mechanics to life, blending technical expertise with creative vision to craft engaging gameplay experiences.

My work has taken me around the world, including a role in Bangkok, Thailand 🇹🇭, where I immersed myself in a new culture and gained fresh perspectives on game development. Now back in Sheffield, I continue to create games that leave a lasting impact on players.

When I'm not programming, I'm exploring new development techniques, playing the latest games, or spending time outdoors with family and friends.

Featured Projects

Procedurally Generated Medieval World (2017)

A procedural world generator created in Unity with extensive use of perlin noise to create a medieval themed world based on the parameters specified within the project. It includes a castle & quaint village within the castle walls, a forest, some farmland and a lake with a boat on top. Terrain features like the hill and the flattening of farmland were created using a brush system inspired by Photoshop which is applied programmically. All assets were created by myself (I'm not an artist!). University Project.
